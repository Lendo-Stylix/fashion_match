\begin{abstract}
Bao cao nay trinh bay qua trinh xay dung va tinh chinh mo hinh \emph{Stylist} -- thanh phan
giao tiep bang ngon ngu tu nhien trong he thong goi y trang phuc OutfitMatch. Trong tam cua
do an la ap dung cac ky thuat hoc sau hien dai: hoc chuyen giao qua mo hinh ngu ngu lon
(LLM) tien huan luyen, tinh chinh tham so thap (LoRA/QLoRA), luong tu hoa 4-bit, va hoc tang
cuong voi phan thuong co the kiem chung (GRPO). Chung toi so sanh bon ung cu vien dua tren
Qwen3-VL-8B, Qwen3.5-9B va Gemma-4-12B, huan luyen tren 11.252 mau hoi thoai tieng Viet,
va chon Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking + LoRA (4-bit) lam mo hinh san xuat voi Tool-F1 dat
\textbf{0.8980}, ti le tuan thu dinh dang \textbf{1.0} va eval loss \textbf{0.5943}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Gioi thieu}
\label{sec:intro}
\subsection{Boi canh va dong luc}
OutfitMatch la he thong goi y trang phuc nhan thuc dang nguoi va dip mac
(\emph{Body \& Occasion-Aware Fashion Recommender}), phat trien trong khuon khoi mon hoc
Deep Learning. He thong hoat dong theo kien truc 4 tang (hinh~\ref{fig:arch}):
(1) Knowledge Base (KB), (2) Stylist, (3) Retrieval, va (4) Personalization (Quiz).
Trong do, tang Stylist dong vai tro \emph{giao dien hoi thoai}: tiep nhan yeu cau tieng
Viet (van ban va/hoac anh) tu nguoi dung, dien giai y dinh (\emph{intent parsing}), quyet
dinh goi cong cu (\emph{tool-calling}) de truy van kho outfit, va cuoi cung giai thich ket
qua bang tieng Viet ma khong bia thong tin.

\subsection{Van de nghien cuu}
Viec dung mot LLM chung (off-the-shelf) lam stylist gap ba thach thuc dac thu:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Anh xa nguon ngu tu nhien sang tu vung kiem soat (\emph{controlled vocabulary}):}
  nguoi dung viet ``di lam'', ``di tiec cuoi'', ``tranh mau sang'' nhung he thong retrieval
  chi chap nhan cac gia tri enum tieng Anh snake\_case (vi du \texttt{office}, \texttt{wedding}, \texttt{elegant}).
  \item \textbf{Chong ao giac (\emph{anti-hallucination}):} mo hinh khong duoc tu bia
  \texttt{outfit\_id} hay gia tien khong ton tai trong KB.
  \item \textbf{Da phuong thuc (\emph{multimodal}):} ho tro nguoi dung gui anh selfie/anh outfit de phan tich dang nguoi.
\end{itemize}
Bao cao nay tap trung vao viec giai quyet cac van de tren bang phuong phap \emph{fine-tuning}
mot LLM da phuong thuc nen tang, thay vi xay dung mo hinh tu dau (von khong kha thi ve du lieu va compute).
\section{Co so ly thuyet}
\label{sec:theory}
\subsection{Mo hinh ngu ngu lon va hoc chuyen giao}
Mo hinh Stylist duoc xay dung tren kien truc Transformer decoder-only tien huan luyen tren
quy mo lon. Thay vi huan luyen tu dau, chung toi tan dung \emph{transfer learning}: trong so
tien huan luyen mang nang luc ngu ngu da ngon ngu va suy luan chung, sau do duoc tinh chinh
tren tap du lieu hoi thoai thoi trang tieng Viet.
Voi bien the da phuong thuc Qwen3-VL, mo hinh bo sung mot bo ma hoa thi giac (ViT --
\emph{Vision Transformer}) noi vao backbone LLM qua co che chieu cheo (cross-attention
projection). Dau vao co the la van ban, anh, hoac ca hai trong cung mot prompt.
\subsection{LoRA va QLoRA -- Tinh chinh tham so thap}
Huan luyen toan bo ($>8$ ty tham so) doi hoi VRAM vuot qua gioi han phan cung (GPU 8--16GB).
Chung toi dung \textbf{QLoRA} (Quantized LoRA) \cite{dettmers2023qlora}, ket hop:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Luong tu hoa 4-bit NF4 + double-quant} giam bo nho trong so nen tang tu
  $\sim 16$GB (bf16) xuong $\sim 4$--$5$GB.
  \item \textbf{LoRA} \cite{hu2021lora}: chi huan luyen cac ma tran he so thap $\Delta W = BA$
  ($\text{rank } r=16$, $\alpha=32$) ghep vao cac projection layers, dong bang trong so goc.
\end{itemize}
So tham so huan luyen chi chiem mot phan nho cua toan bo mo hinh, giup qua trinh huan luyen
chay tren GPU T4 (Kaggle) va RTX 5060 Laptop 8GB (may dev).
\subsection{Hoc tang cuong voi phan thuong co the kiem chung (GRPO)}
Sau SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning), chung toi thu nghiem \textbf{GRPO} (Group Relative Policy
Optimization) \cite{shao2024deepseekmath} -- thuat toan RL on-policy khong dung Reward Model.
GRPO sinh $G$ hoan chinh cho moi prompt, chuan hoa phan thuong theo trung binh va do lech
chuan cua nhom, va toi uu bang PPO-style clipped objective kem KL penalty ($\beta=0.04$):
\begin{equation}
  \hat{A}_i = \frac{r_i - \mathrm{mean}(r_1,\dots,r_G)}{\mathrm{std}(r_1,\dots,r_G)},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{\text{GRPO}} = -\mathbb{E}\!\left[\min\!\left(
  \frac{\pi_\theta(y_i\mid x)}{\pi_{\text{old}}(y_i\mid x)}\hat{A}_i,
  \mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\pi_\theta}{\pi_{\text{old}}},1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon\right)\hat{A}_i
  \right) - \beta\, \mathrm{KL}(\pi_\theta \| \pi_{\text{ref}})\right].
\end{equation}
Phan thuong $r_i$ o day la cac ham quy tac xac dinh (\emph{deterministic scorers}) tren tri
thuc thoi trang, khong can mo hinh phan xet -- dam bao \emph{verifiable reward}.

\subsection{Cong cu truy van (Tool-calling schema)}
Mo hinh khong truy xuat truc tiep ma goi cong cu \texttt{search\_outfits} voi cac tham so co
kieu enum lay tu \texttt{vocab.py} -- nguon duy nhat cua controlled vocabulary:
\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Dinh nghia cong cu search\_outfits (trich xuat tu tools.py).}]
SEARCH_OUTFITS_TOOL = {
  "name": "search_outfits",
  "parameters": {
    "occasion":   enum(list(OCCASION)),     # bat buoc
    "style":      enum(list(STYLE)),
    "body_shape": enum(list(BODY_SHAPE)),
    "skin_tone":  enum(list(SKIN_TONE)),
    "price_max":  integer,                  # VND
    "exclude_colors": array[string],
  },
  "required": ["occasion"],
}
\end{lstlisting}
Dinh dang wire-format chuan hoa:
\texttt{<tool\_call>\{"name":"search\_outfits","arguments":\{...\}\}</tool\_call>}. Mot lop
\emph{validation} kiem tra moi \texttt{outfit\_id} sinh ra co ton tai trong KB va moi tham so
tool co hop le theo schema -- co che chong ao giac cung (\emph{hard guardrail}).
\subsection{Tap du lieu huan luyen}
Tap du lieu cuoi (\emph{final merged dataset}) gom 11.252 mau huan luyen / 348 mau eval,
trong do 1.360 mau huan luyen va 40 mau eval la cac cap tool-calling. Du lieu ket hop ba
nhom hanh vi: \texttt{tool\_calling} / \texttt{tool\_calling\_grounded} (hoc goi dung
field/enum); \texttt{recommend\_explain} (giai thich outfit); \texttt{ask\_missing\_info}
(hoi lai khi thieu thong tin); \texttt{polite\_decline}, \texttt{anti\_hallucination} (tu
choi khi thieu du kien); \texttt{body\_fit}, \texttt{multi\_turn}, \texttt{stylist\_knowledge}
(tu van dang, hoi thoai da luot, kien thuc phoi do). Du lieu sinh tong hop (\emph{synthetic})
bang Gemini Flash cong metadata catalog Viet Nam, chuan hoa sang dinh dang ChatML.

Chay thuc te tren RTX 5060 Laptop 8GB cho thay \emph{gioi han phan cung cung}: o

buoc phan cung.

\section{Thao luan}
\label{sec:discuss}
\subsection{Bai hoc ve do luong chat luong mo hinh}
Voi he thong co \emph{tool contract cung}, eval loss la metric gay hieu lam: mot mo hinh co
loss thap van co the sai enum, bo quen \texttt{<tool\_call>}, hay tra loi prose thay vi JSON.
Benchmark hanh vi (Tool F1, format compliance) phai la tieu chi chon mo hinh chinh.
\subsection{Trade-off da phuong thuc vs. nguon ngu}
Qwen3-VL-8B (Thinking) thang du Qwen3.5-9B text-only vi tinh nang anh nguoi dung la yeu cau
cot loi, va vision tower khong the them sau vao mo hinh text-only. Day la vi du uu tien
\emph{fit voi bai toan san pham} hon toi uu benchmark hep.
\subsection{Han che}
Benchmark 70 mau chua du sau cho regression; GRPO chua chay thanh cong tren compute hien co
do gioi han do dai sinh; du lieu tool-calling hard-case con mong.
\section{Ket luan}
\label{sec:conclude}
Do an hien thuc hoa thanh cong tang Stylist cua OutfitMatch bang fine-tuning LLM da phuong
thuc -- minh hoa chu trinh: transfer learning $\rightarrow$ QLoRA $\rightarrow$ tool-calling
schema $\rightarrow$ verifiable-reward RL $\rightarrow$ benchmark hanh vi. Ket qua: (1) fine-tune
thanh cong 4 adapter QLoRA tren Kaggle, publish len Hugging Face; (2) chon \textbf{Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking
+ LoRA (4-bit)}: Tool F1 = 0.8980, Format = 1.0, Eval loss = 0.5943; (3) thiet ke 6 deterministic
reward functions cho GRPO va phat hien gioi han phan cung thuc te cua RL 8B tren GPU 8GB.
Huong phat trien: tang du lieu hard-case, mo rong benchmark len 200--500 mau, them constrained
decoding / parser repair, va chay GRPO tren T4$\times$2.
